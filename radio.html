Live from Magnuson Park in Seattle, Moon Goose Soup is a radio show on SPACE 101.1FM on Thursday nights at 8pm. What is a Moon Goose Soup, you ask? Honestly, we don’t know either, but listen in with us as we find out. Hosts Sabrina & PJ play a mix of classic and alternative rock, new wave, glam rock, and latin music, intricately blending genres like a well balanced soup … a moon goose soup. We typically keep it upbeat and light. Every week we also discuss a cool new piece of research, combining music with science communication. Tune in to the coolest little radio station in the galaxy every Thursday at 8PM for some Moon Goose Soup!

Pachanga!

Thursdays 11AM on SPACE 101.1FM

What is Pachanga?
Pachanga, a Mexican slang word for party, was the name of my solo radio show on SPACE 101.1FM. I crossed a lot of genres on Pachanga — from 70s/80s glam rock and new wave to more modern indie pop/rock. Each week I had a segment at the first or second break that discussed a cool new piece of science, similar to what we do on Moon Goose Soup now. I discussed space junk crashing down from space, ancient trade routes revealed through isotopes, dinosaurs in Jurassic park, and much more.
